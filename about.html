About Beach Getaway

We help families fall in love with the sea

A real estate agency dedicated to ocean-view rentals for family vacations.

Our story

Born from summers on the shore

Beach Getaway began with a simple idea: finding a great beach house for your family shouldn't be stressful. Our founders grew up spending summers by the ocean and wanted every family to feel that same magic.

Today we partner with homeowners across three beautiful coastlines, matching families with homes that have the views, space and little comforts that turn a trip into a tradition.

Our mission

Make vacation planning easy and joyful

To attract families to the world's most beautiful coastlines, showcase exceptional ocean-view properties, and make every step — from browsing to booking to arriving — simple, convenient and stress-free.
